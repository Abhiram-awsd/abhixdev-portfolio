What it does

Small helper, full day

Focus timerPomodoro sessions with short and long breaks. The countdown sits right under the buddy.
TasksA short list for today, plus the one thing that would make the day a win.
Reminders"Remind me in 40 min", or pick a date and time on the built-in calendar. Once, every day or on weekdays.
Quick addPress Ctrl Alt Space anywhere and type "buy milk" or "call mom in 40 min".
Wellbeing nudgesWater, stretch, eye rest and posture. They wait until your focus session ends.
DaysYour streak, this week against last week, and a Sunday report you can copy.
Three ways to liveIt sits where you drag it, roams the bottom of the screen, or hides in the tray until needed.
Update checkIt tells you when a newer release is out and opens the download page.

Why it is light

Barely there

  • Built on Tauri, so it uses the webview your system already has. The Windows installer is about 1 MB.
  • One small window that is only ever as big as what is on screen.
  • No framework, no bundler and no image files. Each character is drawn from a few dozen lines of shape data.
  • No timers of its own while idle. The shell sends one tick a second.
  • Everything is stored on your own computer. There is no account and no server.
  • The only network request is a once-a-day update check, and you can turn it off.

Install

Get it running

  1. Download the installer for your system from the latest release.
  2. Open it. Deskling appears on your desktop.
  3. Click the buddy to open the panel. Drag it to move it. Right-click it for a trick.
First launchThe builds are not code-signed yet. On Windows, click More info then Run anyway. On macOS, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
# or build from source (needs Rust and Node.js)
npm install
npm run dev
